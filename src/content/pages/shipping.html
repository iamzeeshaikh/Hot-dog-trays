Thank you for shopping with https://hotdogtrays.com/. This Shipping Policy explains how your orders are processed, shipped, and delivered.

1. Production Before Dispatch

Every order is printed to your design, so production comes first. It starts once you approve your digital proof, and standard production takes 7-10 business days. If you are working to an event or opening date, tell us when you order and we will confirm before you pay whether production can meet it.

If we experience delays, we will contact you by email.

2. Free Shipping on All Orders

Shipping is free on every order, whatever the quantity. There is no shipping line added at the end of your quote, so the price you approve already covers delivery to your door.

  • Shipping cost: free on all orders, from the 100-unit minimum upward
  • Destinations: USA, UK, Canada and Australia

3. Delivery Time

Delivery usually takes 5–7 days once your order has shipped. The window starts at dispatch, not at the order date, so plan for production time plus delivery when you set a deadline.

4. Where We Ship

We ship to the USA, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. If your business is somewhere else, contact us before ordering and we will tell you whether we can help.

5. Tracking Information

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number (when available). You can use this number to monitor the delivery progress.

6. Incorrect or Incomplete Addresses

Please check your delivery address, including any suite or unit number, before you approve your order. Orders returned due to:

  • Incorrect address
  • Missing apartment number
  • Undeliverable location

may require additional payment for reshipping.

7. Lost or Missing Packages

If a package is marked as delivered but you did not receive it:

  • Check with neighbors or building management
  • Check around your mailbox or doorstep
  • Allow 24 hours for carrier updates

If the package is still missing, contact us at info@hotdogtrays.com and we will assist you.

8. Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, email us within 14 days of delivery with photos of the package and the trays. Once we confirm the problem, we reprint the affected trays free of charge or refund them, and we cover any shipping involved. Approved refunds are issued within 10 business days to the original payment method.

Because every tray is printed to your artwork, we cannot accept returns for a change of mind. The full returns policy is in section 6 of our Terms and Conditions.

9. International Shipping

Orders delivered to the UK, Canada and Australia may be subject to:

  • Customs fees
  • Import taxes
  • Duties

These charges are the responsibility of the customer. Customs processing may also extend the 5–7 day delivery window.
