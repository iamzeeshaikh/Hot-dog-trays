Thank you for shopping with https://hotdogtrays.com/. This Shipping Policy explains how your orders are processed, shipped, and delivered.

1. Order Processing Time

All orders are processed during regular business hours, Monday through Friday.

  • Standard processing time: 1–3 business days
  • Custom size or custom printing orders: 3–7 business days

If we experience delays, we will contact you by email.

2. Shipping Methods

We work with reliable carriers to ensure safe and timely delivery. Available shipping methods may include:

  • Standard shipping
  • Expedited shipping
  • Priority shipping

Shipping options will appear during checkout based on your location.

3. Shipping Rates

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout. Rates depend on:

  • Order weight
  • Destination address
  • Selected shipping method

Some promotional periods may include free shipping.

4. Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery time begins after your order has been processed. Typical delivery windows:

  • United States: 3–7 business days
  • Canada: 7–12 business days
  • United Kingdom & Australia: 10–15 business days
  • Other international locations: 10–20 business days

Delivery timelines may vary based on customs clearance, carrier delays, or high-season traffic.

5. Tracking Information

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number (when available). You can use this number to monitor the delivery progress.

6. Incorrect or Incomplete Addresses

Please ensure your shipping address is correct at checkout. Orders returned due to:

  • Incorrect address
  • Missing apartment number
  • Undeliverable location

may require additional payment for reshipping.

7. Lost or Missing Packages

If a package is marked as delivered but you did not receive it:

  • Check with neighbors or building management
  • Check around your mailbox or doorstep
  • Allow 24 hours for carrier updates

If the package is still missing, contact us at info@hotdogtrays.com and we will assist you.

8. Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, please email us with photos of the package and the product. We will provide a replacement or help resolve the issue based on the situation.

9. International Shipping

International orders may be subject to:

  • Customs fees
  • Import taxes
  • Duties

These charges are the responsibility of the customer. Customs processing may also extend delivery times.
